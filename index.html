Move with confidence.

Myanmar’s insurance framework combines licensing oversight, sector-specific rules and evolving administrative guidance. A clear view of how these pieces fit together is essential before a product launch, placement or claim strategy.

01

Regulatory oversight

The Financial Regulatory Department (FRD) sits within the Ministry of Planning and Finance. The Insurance Business Law provides for an Insurance Business Supervisory Board, including a role in insurance-business licensing.

02

Core legal instruments

Relevant instruments may include the Insurance Business Law, marine insurance legislation, Social Security Law and rules on motor third-party liability. Applicability depends on the activity and facts.

MARKET ENTRY · 04 MIN READ

What are the foreign ownership limits for insurance businesses?

There is no safe universal percentage to quote without checking the proposed license class, company structure and current FRD approvals. Foreign-insurer entry was liberalised through a 2019 process, but the exact route and permissions are category-specific.

PLACEMENT · 03 MIN READ

How do direct insurance and reinsurance differ?

Direct insurance transfers risk from the policyholder to an insurer; reinsurance transfers part of an insurer’s accepted risk onward. Myanmar’s Insurance Business Law addresses licensing and reinsurance programs, and contemplates approval for direct insurance abroad in specified circumstances. Do not assume cross-border placement is unrestricted.

OBLIGATIONS · 03 MIN READ

Is auto or life insurance mandatory in Myanmar?

Myanma Insurance describes motor third-party liability insurance as provided by law. That should not be confused with comprehensive motor cover. The public sources linked here do not establish a blanket life-insurance requirement for every business or person; check employment, sector and transaction-specific rules.

OBLIGATIONS · 02 MIN READ

How does social security fit into an insurance review?

Social security obligations arise under a separate statutory framework and should be reviewed alongside, not substituted for, commercial insurance cover. Confirm employer and worker obligations with current rules and the relevant authorities.
